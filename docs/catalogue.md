# Before and after

More of the game with and without **FFTA2 One Screen**. In every picture, the left side is
the unmodified game on the DS's two screens (shown at 2x), and the right side is the same
moment with the mod's Steam Deck patch, on one 1280x800 screen. Each pair comes from the
same savestate.

## Scions

The Scions' summons are the only animations the game draws across both screens at once. With
the mod they play as one tall picture on the one screen.

![Belias's summon: on the DS its picture runs across both screens; with the mod it plays whole on one screen](pictures/belias.webp)
